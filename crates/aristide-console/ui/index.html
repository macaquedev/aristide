MIDI input

Press Listen, then play your keyboard's lowest key and its highest: that names the port and channel, and measures how wide the keyboard is. Keys past the sample set's own compass are filled in by repitching the nearest pipe; keys outside the measured range stay silent.

Compass

A manual can be told to reach further than its sample set's own keys; the extra notes are filled in by repitching the nearest pipe. Native goes back to what the set declares. Note names, C4 being middle C — a 5-octave manual is C2–C7.

Bindings · this organ

Any MIDI message or computer key can be bound to anything the console can do — draw a stop, work a coupler, nudge the pitch, silence everything. A message bound this way does not also play a note.
